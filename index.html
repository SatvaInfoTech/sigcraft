              How to add to Gmail / Outlook:
            

            
    
              
  1. Click the blue "Copy Signature" button above.
    2. 
              
  2. In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → General → Signature (or View all Outlook settings → Compose and reply).
    3. 
              
  3. Click "Create new" and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac).
    4. 
              
  4. Scroll down and click "Save Changes".
    5. 
            

          


          
          

            

              How to add to Mozilla Thunderbird:
            

            
    
              
  1. In Thunderbird, open Account Settings and select your email account.
    2. 
              
  2. Option A (Easiest): Click "Download" above to save email-signature.html. In Thunderbird, check  "Attach the signature from a file instead", click Choose..., and pick the file.
    3. 
              
  3. Option B (Direct HTML): Click "View HTML" → "Copy Code" above. In Thunderbird, check  "Use HTML" and paste into the Signature text box.
    4. 
            

          


          
          

            

              How to add to Apple Mail:
            

            
    
              
  1. Click "Copy Signature" above.
    2. 
              
  2. In Apple Mail, open Settings (Preferences) → Signatures.
    3. 
              
  3. Click + to add a signature, uncheck "Always match my default message font", and press Cmd+V.
    4.
